About Me

Hey there! I’m Catherine. 🌻

I’m a Houston native and a recent grad from Washington University in St. Louis (Class of 2026) with a BA in Arts. I’m naturally curious and always chasing the next adventure—whether that's wandering through a new city, studying a fascinating subject, or getting lost in a good book.

I’m a huge foodie and I absolutely love exploring different cultures through their cuisines! Learning about the world through food and shared experiences is one of my favorite things.

At my core, I’m an artist, designer, and writer. I pour my heart into creating designs and art that build community and give people a way to express their emotions. For me, creativity is all about making people feel seen and understood.

"I can't wait to connect and collaborate with others who challenge and inspire me. Let's create something beautiful together!"
