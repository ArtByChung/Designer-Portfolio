About Me

Hi, I'm Catherine. 🌻

I'm a designer, writer, and operations-minded thinker. I'm the person who keeps projects moving, keeps stakeholders in the loop, and makes sure every detail is right the first time.

My background spans operations at a Fortune 500 healthcare company and a summer in investment banking at a global bank. In all my experiences, I take a messy pile of information, figure out what it's actually telling us, and build a better process behind it. I bring that exact same analytical rigor to my design work.

I care deeply about creating visuals that build community and help people express themselves, and I always ground that creativity in strategy and clean execution.

Beyond design, I volunteer in Houston and am a huge foodie. Our local food scene represents over 70 different cultures, and I'm on a very serious mission to try them all. Maybe I should track that in a spreadsheet...
